Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta Simple Recipe

Description

Learn to cook delicous Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta with this simple recipe

Ingredients

  • Fettuccine
  • Shrimp
  • Clove Garlic
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Butter
  • Cooking Oil
  • Parsley

Steps

  1. Clean shrimp
  2. Cook garlic in oil over medium heat
  3. Melt butter and cook shrimp for 4-6 minutes
  4. Transfer fettuccine into a pan
  5. Add shrimp and garnish with parsley
